# Ludo Ladder

**Ludo and Snakes & Ladders for 2–4 players.**

[Play now](https://nipunkakkar.github.io/ludo-ladder/)

Play together on one device or create a private online room. Invite friends with a room code or link.

- **Ludo:** Race your tokens home and capture opponents.
- **Snakes & Ladders:** Climb ladders, slide down snakes and reach the finish.

Includes animated boards, music and sound effects. Keep the host's tab open during online games.
